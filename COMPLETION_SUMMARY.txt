🎮 NEW FEATURES ADDED

Drag & Rotate
  └─ Click and drag the globe to spin it smoothly in 3D
     • Accurate mouse tracking
     • Smooth rotation following cursor
     • No jitter or stuttering

Momentum Effect
  └─ Globe continues rotating after you release
     • Damping factor: 0.95 per frame
     • Gradual deceleration
     • Feels natural and responsive

Hover Detection
  └─ See city names when hovering over major locations
     • Real-time raycasting detection
     • Tooltip appears at bottom-left
     • Works on 6 major world cities

Click Ripples
  └─ Beautiful water ripple effect when clicking
     • Originates from click point
     • Expands outward in concentric circles
     • Fades over 1.1 seconds
     • Multiple ripples can coexist

Auto-Rotation
  └─ Gentle rotation when not interacting
     • Very slow speed (0.0003 rad/frame)
     • Automatically pauses during drag
     • Resumes smoothly after release

Zoom Control
  └─ Mouse wheel to zoom in/out
     • Camera distance: 1.5 to 4.0
     • Smooth zoom transitions
     • Maintains interaction quality

Star Background
  └─ 400+ stars in procedurally generated starfield
     • Subtle twinkling effect
     • Adds depth and atmosphere
     • Not distracting or overwhelming

Atmospheric Glow
  └─ Subtle cyan halo around Earth
     • Rim lighting effect
     • Scientific aesthetic
     • Enhances 3D appearance

              Clear cache and refresh

  └─ Solution: Close other browser tabs

              Reduce screen resolution
